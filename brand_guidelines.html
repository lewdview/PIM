SECTION 01

VISION & THE RETENTION THESIS

PIM is a systems product bridging a high-precision HTML5 Canvas 3-lane rhythm game with an audio-reactive digital collectible card engine under an uncompromising technical brutalist aesthetic.

THE RETENTION THESIS

MUSIC UNLOCKS GAMEPLAY → GAMEPLAY UNLOCKS OWNERSHIP → OWNERSHIP UNLOCKS STATUS

01. MUSIC

Engages players with 365 daily song drops (one for every day of the calendar year) accessed via deep links and chapter maps.

02. GAMEPLAY

High accuracy (>70% Bronze to 100% Platinum) earns collectible card drops containing session stems, registry proofs, and card burn assets.

03. OWNERSHIP

Players forge duplicates, elevate global prestige scores, showcase streaks, unlock 1-of-1 First Discoverer stamps, and bind to Base EVM wallets.

THE THREE SIMULTANEOUS ECONOMIES

THE SKILL ECONOMY

Governed by millisecond timing windows, swipe precision, hold ribbon tracking, and unbroken combo multipliers up to 5x.

THE SCARCITY ECONOMY

Powered by global hard supply caps, rarity tiers (Common to Mythic), mintable vs. gameplay splits, and card burning sinks.

THE SOCIAL ECONOMY

Expressed through collection prestige points, global leaderboard telemetry, replay ghosts, and 1-of-1 First Discoverer certifications.

CORE AESTHETIC DIRECTIVE
PIM rejects generic, soft, rounded SaaS interfaces in favor of Technical Brutalism meets High-Fidelity Cyber Neon. Think military HUDs, tactile synthesizer chassis, and glowing terminal telemetry.

THE FORGE OPERATIONS

🔥 CARD BURNING

Deconstruct duplicate cards into V⚡ tokens to power targeted pulls and rarity upgrades.

⚡ TARGETED REQUISITION

Spend 500 V⚡ to guarantee pull any specific card from the 365-day release catalog.

• Operative / Collector (Not "User" / "Customer")
• TH3V4ULT / The Codex (Not "Library" / "Catalog")
• Rip Packs / Daily Drop (Not "Loot Box" / "Reward")
• Burn in The Forge (Not "Recycle" / "Sell")

• Pay-to-win mechanics (Skill drives ownership)

• Unattributed audio degradation (Always reflect misses in DSP)
